Enterprise FAQ

Scenario Questions & Answers

Do recipients need to install any app to scan TapDID NFC cards or QR codes? +

No! TapDID uses universal standard NFC protocols natively supported by iPhone (iOS 13+) and Android phones. A simple tap instantly opens your profile or contact card in their phone's native browser.

Can we update employee or product details after the physical card is printed? +

Yes, 100%! Because TapDID uses dynamic cloud identity profiles, you can update designations, phone numbers, portfolio links, or access clearance levels anytime in real time without re-printing the physical card.

How does TapDID prevent physical asset NFC tags from being cloned? +

TapDID asset tags utilize cryptographic tamper-proof chips with dynamic rolling counter keys (SUN technology). Each scan generates a unique one-time URL hash that cannot be cloned or copied.

Can TapDID integrate with our company's existing HRMS or access control doors? +

Yes. TapDID provides REST APIs and Webhook endpoints that integrate seamlessly with corporate HRMS platforms, CRM software (HubSpot, Salesforce), and physical RFID/NFC door readers.
